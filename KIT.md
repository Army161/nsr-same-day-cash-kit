# North Star Same-Day Cash Offer Kit

Instant digital delivery. One legal offer, one checkout, one proof rule.

This kit is original operating material from North Star Revenue. It is not financial advice, not a guarantee of sales, and not a trading system. A buyer still has to decide.

## The only scoreboard

Profit is settled net USD after fees and refunds. A link, a like, a waitlist, and a plan are not profit.

Stop line used to build this kit: 100.00 USD net. One card sale at 109 USD nets about 105.54 after a 2.9% + 0.30 fee. Do not count a charge on your own card.

## 45-minute ship sequence

1. Name the payer in one sentence. Role, context, budget. If you cannot name them, you do not have an offer.
2. Name the job they already spend time or money on this week.
3. Write one sentence they can repeat: who it is for, what they get, when it arrives.
4. Price at 109 USD if one sale must clear 100 net. Price at 29 only if you can realistically collect four external sales.
5. Build the file before the checkout. Delivery is a URL or file that exists the moment payment succeeds.
6. Open one payment link. Card payments must already be enabled on the account.
7. Post once on a channel you already use. No bulk DMs. No bought reviews. No ad spend used to fake demand.
8. Poll the rail. Pending is not profit. Refunds subtract.

## Offer sentence template

For [payer] who [painful current alternative], this is a [file] they can use in [timebox], delivered the moment checkout succeeds, for [price].

## What to sell on day one

Sell a finished file, not a promise of coaching.

- A checklist they can run today
- A prompt pack they can paste today
- A one-page audit with a named next action

Do not sell guaranteed returns, signals, or done-for-you access you cannot actually deliver in the same session.

## Ledger row that counts

kind: sale, payout, commission, cashback, or realized_trade
status: settled
gross: amount the buyer paid
fees: processor fee
evidence: charge id from the processor
banned: self_purchase, circular_transfer, projected, test_mode

## Refusal list

Do not clear the floor with fraud, fake invoices, card testing, wash trades, scraped lists, purchased reviews, or a charge on your own card.

## After the first settled sale

Deliver the file. Leave the link up. Do not add a second product until the first one can actually be bought.
